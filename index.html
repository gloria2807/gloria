I'm Gloria, a product designer and frontend developer who likes working where thoughtful product decisions meet expressive visual design.

HOW I THINK

Clarity before decoration

Pretty is useful when it makes the product easier to understand. Simplicity is best when you put the user in mind.

Details are not decoration

Motion, copy, spacing and tiny states shape how a product feels.

Design should survive contact with code

I care about what happens when the polished Figma frame becomes a real interface.
